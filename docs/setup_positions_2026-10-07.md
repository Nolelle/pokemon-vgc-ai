## Observations (why the setup calls lost)

1. **The setup move almost always loses the one-turn exchange, and not by a hair.** Searched
   at identical dice, the best setup order trails the pick by 20-70 points in positions
   1, 2, 3, 4 and 7, by 70-165 in 5, and by 110-185 in 6 (Water Spout at full HP). The dice check re-rolls the sampled
   Showdown futures 5 times and the setup order never wins (0/5 in all 24 position x exact
   version cells), so this is not sampling noise. The exact judge scores HP and Pokemon
   alive after ONE turn (no continuation), so Tailwind/Trick Room/terrain pay only through
   the field-control leaf. That leaf is worth +20 to +47 for TR/Tailwind (B, C) and closes
   about half the gap: position 3 C scores Fake Out + Tailwind 15.4 (field +21.6) vs 40.9

   +20.7 for Indeedee TR + Hatterene Protect. The field term moves B/C toward Trick Room in
   position 1 (A never sets it) but does not make the owner's exact line win.

2. **The shortlist is not what hides the owner's call here.** In positions 3, 5 and 6 the
   expected order is cut by the myopic top-10 (rank 19-22 of 58-110; for D0/D1 fast rank
   19-22, not in the judged top 6). Forcing it into the search shows it would not have won

   +1..+15 vs +32..+41). So widening the shortlist alone would change nothing in these
   eight positions; the value function is what disagrees with the owner.
