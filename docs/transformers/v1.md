# Transformer v1 (`replica_m128_l4_37101`)

> **Takeaway:** `v1` was our first working end-to-end Transformer (an AI model that weighs how every Pokémon and move on the board relates to every other one) trained by playing 500,000 games against itself. Paired with live lookahead search, it went **17–13 (56.7%)** on the public Regulation M-C ladder with **zero errors**, peaking at **1255 Elo** — proving the entire self-play + search pipeline worked from start to finish.

## 2. What We Built & Changed

- **MikuMiku-inspired self-play + search pilot:** Earlier phases ran Pokémon Showdown's Node.js simulator one turn at a time, which was far too slow for large-scale self-play (training an AI by having it play thousands of games against past versions of itself). We adopted `pikapro`, a fast Rust battle simulator.
- **Engine parity fixes (600/600 exact match):** We found and fixed two RNG-order bugs in `pikapro 0.1.0` (forced-switch update order and fainted status speed-tie sorting) plus Champions `Last Resort` disabling, verified **600/600** full-game parity against Showdown, and upstreamed the fixes into official `pikapro 0.2.0`.
- **Size bake-off (2,048 games):** Before scaling up, we compared Medium (`128×4`, 1.16M params) against Full (`268×12`, 8.69M params). Medium was ~3× faster (112s vs 331s), much steadier (`approx_kl` 0.027 vs 0.148 — meaning its strategy updates didn't swing wildly), and beat the `maxpower` baseline more often (45% vs 36%).
- **Parallel trainer (`selfplay/train_replication_league.py`):** Added multi-worker rollouts, `desktop`/`afk` profiles, atomic `--resume`, periodic holdout evaluation, and Weights & Biases (W&B — an experiment tracking dashboard) logging.
- **Live lookahead upgrade (`replica-search`):** Built a persistent reusable `LiveExactMirror` over Showdown, batched leaf value evaluation, and mixed-strategy sampling for public ladder play (`--policy-mode replica-search`).

## 3. Training Schedule & Hyperparameters

`v1` was trained in four staged phases (resuming the same checkpoint while lowering the learning rate — the step size the model takes when updating its weights — and growing the batch of games per update):

| Phase | Games | Profile | Learning Rate | Games / Update | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 3a** | 0 -> 20,000 | `afk` | `3e-4` | 64 | ~10.3 min (~25–30 g/s); plateaued as `approx_kl` rose to `0.081` |
| **Phase 3b** | 20,000 -> 60,000 | `desktop` | `1e-4` | 256 | Stabilized updates; `maxpower` reached 80–85% |
| **Phase 3c** | 60,000 -> 150,000 | `afk` | `5e-5` | 256 | Added `vgc` 2-ply search to holdout evals |
| **Phase 3d** | 150,000 -> 500,000 | `afk` | `2e-5` | 512 | Finished at 500k games (~37 g/s late average) |

- **Optimizer / PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization — a reinforcement learning method that limits how far the bot's strategy can jump in one step):** `batch_size=64`, `epochs=3` (passes over each batch of collected games), `league_size=24` historical snapshots, `snapshot_games=256` initially.
- **Throughput:** Started at `12.9 games/sec` (`247 decisions/sec`), peaked at `40.7 games/sec` (`509 decisions/sec`), averaged **`37.2 games/sec`** over the last 50 updates.

## 6. Key Lessons & What Drove v2

- **What worked:** Self-play on `pikapro 0.2.0` + lookahead search cleanly scaled from zero game knowledge to a ~1200–1250 Elo ladder player with zero crashes or illegal moves.
- **Bottlenecks found:**
  1. Model capacity (`128×4`, 1.16M params) and team diversity (226 teams) capped how many matchups `v1` could learn.
  2. Manual learning-rate drops between phases were clunky — needed an automatic cosine learning-rate schedule.
  3. High late `approx_kl` (`0.037`) and small batch sizes (`batch_size=64`) made late value calibration noisy (`value_loss` drifted up to `0.735`).
