# Transformer v3 (`rust_v3_m192_l6_379t`)

> **Takeaway:** `v3` was our breakthrough generation. By moving self-play directly into Rust `VecEnv` on the Apple M4 Pro GPU and making the Transformer truly move-order and team-slot invariant, we scaled to **3,000,004 games in 8.9 hours**. Plain `v3` doubled `v1`'s win rate against our 2-ply `vgc` bot (**64.0% avg, 72.5% peak**), and live on the public ladder it jumped **+91 Elo** to a **1346 peak**.

## 2. What We Built & Changed from v2

- **Native Rust `VecEnv` + `rich` encoder (`patches/pikapro/0001-rich-encoder-and-patch-public.patch`):** Eliminated Python multiprocessing bottlenecks by stepping 256 battles at once inside Rust and running both rollouts and PPO updates on MPS GPU (~95–104 games/sec, **~4.5× faster than `v2`**).
- **Rich fog-safe board features (1,028 floats):** Added explicit turn timers (weather, Trick Room, terrains, Tailwind, screens), `protect_counter`, Fake Out `first_turn`, `turns_active`, PP/disabled, volatiles, and strictly public unbrought opponent preview rows (zero hidden-info leaks).
- **Audit #2 — Move-order & team-slot equivariance (`policy_version=2`):** Caught a critical bug where summing move embeddings blinded the model to which move sat in slots 1–4. Replaced blind linear heads with **semantic move heads** (scoring each move directly from its own embedding + PP/disabled state), **semantic switch heads**, and **semantic Slot 0 -> Slot 1 conditioning** (Slot 1 sees the exact move/switch Slot 0 picked). Verified bit-exact invariance to shuffling moves or bench order.
- **Native Rust live lookahead (`rust-search`):** Added `Battle.patch_public` + `Battle.fork()` so live lookahead runs directly in Rust instead of spawning Node.js Showdown processes.
- **Clean 379-team pool & stable PPO math:** Removed all 369 stale M-B / synthetic teams; added GAE (`gamma=0.99, gae_lambda=0.95`), BCE win-probability value head, `batch_size=512`, `games_per_update=1024`, `epochs=2`, `target_kl=0.025`, and 10% `simple_heuristics` anchor opponents.

## 6. Post-Mortem & What Drove v3b

1. **1-turn Protect stall trap (fixed immediately):** In the first 30 live games, 1-turn search clicked Protect 49 times in 14 losses (throwing a won 2v1 by clicking Protect 5 turns straight). Upgrading to **2-turn regularized lookahead** fixed it cleanly (0 stall loops in the next 60 live games).
2. **Meta shift to `Pelipper + Grimmsnarl + Archaludon + Charizard`:** Nearly half of 1350+ ladder games were against this Rain/Screens core, which was underrepresented in our 379-team pool. That directly motivated building the fresh 696-team November meta pool and warm-starting **`v3b`**.
