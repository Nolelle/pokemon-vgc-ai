# Complete Champions Reg M-B mechanics catalogue

Generated and audited: 2026-08-27

## What "complete" means here

The authoritative machine-readable inventory is
`data/champions/mechanics_catalog.json`. It is generated by
`tools/export_mechanics_catalog.mjs` from Pokemon Showdown's **fully merged** Champions
Dex. That includes both the custom Champions files and every ordinary Gen 9 mechanic
they inherit.

The inventory currently contains:

- 355 legal species and battle forms, representing 264 base species;
- 496 legal moves learnable by at least one legal species;
- 208 abilities used by at least one legal species or form;
- 148 legal held items;
- 35 global conditions plus every move-defined volatile condition;
- every active format rule, engine callback, declarative move effect, targeting mode,
  and move/ability/item flag attached to those legal entities.

The JSON contains the exact entity lists. This document groups that exhaustive surface
into mechanics a decision system must understand. A mechanic is not "understood" merely
because its name earns a score bonus. Understanding requires the relevant state, legal
transitions, probabilities, ordering, and interactions.

## 1. Format, team construction, and information

- Doubles battle: two active Pokemon per side.
- Six Pokemon registered; four selected for the battle; two chosen as leads.
- Species Clause and one-of-each Item Clause.
- Level adjusted to 50.
- Champions Stat Points: at most 32 per stat and 66 total; fixed 31 Individual Values.
- Custom legal species, forms, learnsets, abilities, and 148-item pool.
- Mythical and Restricted Legendary tags banned.
- Open Team Sheets reveal species, moves, items, and abilities.
- Nature and Stat Point spread remain hidden.
- Mega Evolution is enabled and uses the Champions form/item roster.
- Terastallization, Dynamax, Gigantamax, Z-Moves, and Primal Reversion are unavailable
  in this format even though generic inherited engine hooks may still exist.
- No Sleep Moves Clause, one-hit-knockout clause, or evasion clause is present.
- Battle timer, first-turn timer, grace, per-turn additions, timeout choice, and disconnect
  bank are format rules, even though they do not change move outcomes.

## 2. Pokemon identity and persistent state

- Species, base species, form, apparent form, nickname, gender, level, shiny state.
- Base types, current types, added/removed types, type-changing effects.
- Base stats, final stats, nature, Stat Points, and all seven stages: Attack, Defense,
  Special Attack, Special Defense, Speed, accuracy, and evasion.
- Current health, maximum health, health fraction, fainted state, and replacement state.
- Weight and effects that change weight.
- Current ability, base ability, temporary/replaced/suppressed ability, and ability state.
- Current item, consumed/removed/changed item, item state, berry eligibility, and item
  suppression such as Klutz, Magic Room, Embargo, or Unnerve.
- Four move slots, current and maximum Power Points, disabled moves, copied/replaced
  moves, and move-calling restrictions.
- Major status and its internal timer/counter.
- Arbitrary volatile effects and their timers, sources, linked targets, and restart rules.
- Transform, Illusion, Substitute, Mega form, and other form-change state.
- Last move selected, last move executed, last successful move, last damage, attackers,
  move result, hurt-this-turn, newly switched, and times attacked.
- Protect-chain counter, charge/preparation state, recharge state, trapping state, forced
  switch state, and choice lock.

## 3. Turn and action ordering

- Request phase, team preview, forced replacement, normal choice, and wait state.
- Switching before ordinary moves, with Pursuit-like and switch-interception exceptions.
- Mega Evolution timing before the selected move and its immediate stat/ability/weather
  consequences.
- Move priority, ability/item priority changes, fractional priority, and priority blocks.
- Effective Speed after stages, paralysis, items, abilities, Tailwind, weather, and rooms.
- Trick Room order reversal without integer-underflow shortcuts.
- Speed-tie random choice.
- Action invalidation if the actor faints, switches, flinches, sleeps, freezes, becomes
  fully paralyzed, recharges, is commanded, or otherwise cannot act.
- Move selection restrictions from Taunt, Encore, Disable, Torment-like effects, choice
  lock, Assault-style restrictions where legal, Imprison, Heal Block, Gravity, and PP.
- Random-number use and branch order; random calls are themselves part of exact behavior.

## 4. Targeting and doubles topology

- Self, adjacent ally, ally-or-self, adjacent foe, any adjacent Pokemon, random foe,
  both foes, all adjacent Pokemon, ally side, foe side, all allies, whole field, and
  scripted targets.
- Target validity after a switch or faint.
- Retargeting when the chosen target is gone.
- Follow Me and Rage Powder redirection, including powder immunities.
- Lightning Rod and Storm Drain style ability redirection.
- Ally targeting and friendly fire.
- Spread-move damage reduction based on the number of targets actually hit.
- Wide Guard, Quick Guard, Crafty Shield, Mat Block, Protect-family moves, Feint, and
  moves that partially or fully bypass protection.
- Adjacency-sensitive effects, even though ordinary two-on-two battles keep all active
  slots adjacent.

## 5. Accuracy, evasion, and move success

- Base accuracy, always-hit moves, accuracy/evasion stages, item/ability modifiers, and
  field modifiers.
- Separate hit checks for multiaccuracy moves and multi-hit moves where applicable.
- Semi-invulnerable states such as Fly/Dig/Dive-style turns and moves that can reach them.
- Type immunity, ability immunity, terrain immunity, status immunity, powder/sound/
  bullet/wind/dance/contact classifications, and immunity bypass.
- Substitute, Safeguard, Mist, terrain, Aroma Veil-like protection, and reflection by
  Magic Coat/Magic Bounce.
- Generic failure conditions: missing prerequisites, duplicate effects, no valid target,
  full health, empty item, wrong weather/terrain/form, and move-specific callbacks.

## 6. Damage pipeline

- Move category, type, base power, attacking stat, defending stat, and stage modifiers.
- Move- or ability-driven type conversion such as Pixilate.
- Variable base power and fixed-damage callbacks.
- Level-based base damage and Champions rounding/truncation rules.
- Burn, screens, weather, terrain, spread penalty, critical hits, random damage roll,
  same-type attack bonus, type effectiveness, immunity, and final modifiers.
- Ability and item modifiers on the user, target, allies, foes, source, or whole field.
- Critical-hit ratio, guaranteed critical hits, critical immunity, and stage ignoring.
- One-hit knockout moves: Fissure, Guillotine, Horn Drill, and Sheer Cold are present and
  legal for at least one species because this format has no one-hit-knockout clause.
- Counter/Mirror Coat/Metal Burst retaliation; Endeavor and Final Gambit; level/fixed
  damage; Super Fang-style fractional damage.
- Weight-, Speed-, health-, friendship-, status-, boost-, item-, target-state-, and
  previous-action-dependent power.
- Multi-hit count distributions, Population Bomb/Triple Axel-style per-hit accuracy,
  Parental Bond, Beat Up participants, and repeated item/ability/contact triggers.
- Damage caps/floors, Focus Sash, Sturdy, Disguise, Ice Face, Endure, False Swipe, and
  other survival effects.

## 7. Damage aftermath

- Recoil, crash damage, Struggle recoil, Mind Blown-style recoil, and Life Orb.
- Drain, Big Root-like changes, Liquid Ooze-like reversal, and healing prevention.
- Contact reactions such as Rough Skin, Rocky Helmet, Static, Flame Body, and abilities
  that alter contact.
- Damaging-hit and source-damaging-hit callbacks.
- Secondary effects, their independent chances, Sheer Force-style removal, Shield Dust,
  Serene Grace, Covert-style protection where legal, and multi-hit trigger timing.
- Self stat changes, target stat changes, status, flinch, item changes, ability changes,
  volatile effects, and switching caused after damage.
- Faint events, simultaneous faint ordering, Soul-Heart/Moxie-like triggers, Destiny
  Bond, Aftermath, and replacement requests.

## 8. Major status conditions

- Burn: physical damage reduction, end-turn damage, immunity, application and cure.
- Paralysis: Speed reduction plus the Champions-specific one-in-eight action denial.
- Poison: end-turn damage, immunity, application and cure.
- Toxic poison: increasing counter, reset rules, end-turn damage, application and cure.
- Sleep: Champions duration of two or three action opportunities, Early Bird, Sleep
  Talk/Snore, wake-up, Rest, terrain, berries, abilities, powder rules, and allies.
- Freeze: Champions three-step timer plus one-in-four early thaw, defrosting moves,
  Fire-move interactions, form changes, application and cure.

## 9. Volatile and action-control conditions

- Confusion, self-hit calculation, duration, cure, and Own Tempo-like protection.
- Flinch and Fake Out, Inner Focus-like protection, and flinch-trigger abilities.
- Taunt, Encore, Disable, choice lock, Imprison, recharge, and two-turn locks.
- Yawn/drowsy delayed sleep.
- Substitute health and interaction matrix.
- Leech Seed, Curse, Nightmare, Salt Cure-like residuals, trapping and partial trapping.
- Infatuation, Torment, Heal Block, Embargo, Perish Song, Destiny Bond, Grudge, and
  move-specific volatiles.
- Charge moves, semi-invulnerable turns, locked multi-turn moves, Rollout storage, and
  recharge moves.
- Protect/stall chain and move-specific shield retaliation.
- Ability- and move-created linked conditions, restart behavior, copying, Baton Pass,
  Haze/Clear Smog, and switching cleanup.

The exact move-defined condition callback paths and affected move IDs are enumerated
under `mechanics_surface.callbacks.moves` in the JSON catalogue; this avoids a manual
list silently omitting a unique volatile.

## 17. End-of-turn and event ordering

- Win, loss, tie, endless-battle prevention, and no-available-Pokemon resolution.

## 18. Knowledge and uncertainty

- Our team, moves, item, ability, nature, Stat Points, current PP, and selected four are
  private facts available to our agent.
- Open Team Sheets reveal the opponent's species, move names, items, and abilities, but
  not nature or Stat Points.
- The opponent's selected four and back two are hidden until revealed.
- Exact random state, future choices, Speed ties, damage rolls, accuracy rolls, secondary
  rolls, wake/thaw rolls, and hidden spreads require branches or probability beliefs;
  they must never be filled with simulator-private truth.
