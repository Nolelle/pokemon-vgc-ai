### Psyspam Sand

- **Plan:** Indeedee-M sets Psychic Terrain, which consumes Sneasler's Psychic Seed (Unburden doubles its Speed) and powers Expanding Force. The back half is a sand mode (Mega Tyranitar, Sand Rush Excadrill) to fight opposing weather, mainly Charizard.
- **Leads:** Indeedee-M + Sneasler by default. Tyranitar + Excadrill as the sand mode; Corviknight Tailwind against fast teams.
- **Win conditions:** early damage from Sneasler and Expanding Force; Excadrill cleaning under sand.
- **Bot notes:** Psychic Terrain blocks Fake Out from Rillaboom and Incineroar on grounded targets. Earthquake is safe next to Corviknight (Flying) and Hydreigon (Levitate). Hydreigon's Haban Berry survives Garchomp's Dragon Claw and KOs back with Draco Meteor. Acrobatics is for the Sneasler mirror. Three teammates are weak to Ground, so Garchomp is the main threat.
