### Gardevoir Psyspam

- **Plan:** three modes the opponent cannot cover at once: Indeedee-F Follow Me + Mega Gardevoir Expanding Force; Indeedee-F + Psychic Seed Sneasler; Charizard-Y + Venusaur sun.
- **Leads:** Indeedee-F + Gardevoir (main). Also Indeedee-F + Sneasler, Charizard + Venusaur, or Basculegion + Gardevoir and Flip Turn into a stronger mode.
- **Win conditions:** Expanding Force spam once the opponent's Dark and Steel types are gone; Basculegion as the late-game cleaner.
- **Bot notes:** Indeedee is Relaxed (slow) on purpose: it moves after a faster Rillaboom, so its Psychic Terrain replaces their Grassy Terrain. Imprison stops opposing Trick Room. Sneasler has no Protect: Rock Tomb for speed control and Charizard, Throat Chop for Basculegion and Gholdengo, Gunk Shot for Floette, Close Combat to remove Dark and Steel types that wall Gardevoir. Psychic Terrain protects Basculegion from Sucker Punch.
