### Terrain Pulse Blastoise

- **Plan:** Mega Blastoise (Mega Launcher) uses Terrain Pulse: Psychic-type in Indeedee's terrain, Grass-type in Rillaboom's, with Shell Smash and Water Spout. A balance team with Fake Out and Follow Me support.
- **Leads:** Indeedee-F + Blastoise (Follow Me covers Shell Smash); Rillaboom or Incineroar Fake Out + Blastoise; Rillaboom + Floette (Flower Veil stops Intimidate and status on Grass types).
- **Win conditions:** a +2 Blastoise sweep; Floette Calm Mind.
- **Bot notes:** Grass Terrain Pulse hits opposing Water types such as Basculegion; Psychic Terrain Pulse KOs Sneasler. Floette covers Dragons (Mega Dragonite), which Blastoise cannot hit super effectively. Sneasler's Rock Tomb is for Charizard. Which of Blastoise or Floette Mega Evolves is decided in battle.
