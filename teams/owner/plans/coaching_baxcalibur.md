### Coaching Baxcalibur

- **Plan:** Grassy Terrain from Rillaboom weakens Earthquake into Kingambit; Fake Out lets Kingambit Swords Dance; Sneasler's Coaching raises its partner's Attack and Defense, and four teammates are physical attackers that benefit. Mega Baxcalibur handles Garchomp; Froslass adds Aurora Veil and snow.
- **Leads:** Fake Out (Rillaboom or Sneasler) + Kingambit to set up; Sneasler Coaching + Rillaboom; Froslass Aurora Veil + Baxcalibur.
- **Win conditions:** boosted Kingambit Sucker Punch; Baxcalibur after a Scale Shot Speed boost; Basculegion Last Respects late.
- **Bot notes:** one Scale Shot boost lets Baxcalibur outspeed Mega Staraptor and Timid Floette, but it can miss. Fighting types (Mega Staraptor) are the team's main weakness; Froslass helps there. Which of Baxcalibur or Froslass Mega Evolves is decided in battle.
