### Salamence TW

- **Plan:** Tailwind offense built around Mega Salamence. It sets Tailwind and gives Intimidate support while Basculegion, Garchomp, Kingambit and Floette attack under it.
- **Leads:** Salamence + Sneasler: Fake Out lets Salamence set Tailwind; Feint breaks Protect in Tailwind mirrors.
- **Win conditions:** double attacks under Tailwind; Floette and Garchomp as fast closers.
- **Bot notes:** Sneasler covers the Ice, Steel and Fairy types that threaten Salamence, and Close Combat removes Kingambit. Kingambit is built to survive Floette's Light of Ruin because the team has no Fairy resist. Which of Salamence or Floette Mega Evolves is decided in battle.
