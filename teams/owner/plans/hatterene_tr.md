### Hatterene TR

- **Plan:** hard Trick Room. Indeedee-F redirects with Follow Me while Hatterene or Farigiraf sets Trick Room; then slow Expanding Force and Torkoal's sun-boosted Eruption.
- **Leads:** Indeedee-F + Hatterene (Follow Me + Trick Room); Incineroar + Farigiraf; Charizard + Farigiraf as the fast mode.
- **Win conditions:** Torkoal Eruption and Hatterene Expanding Force under Trick Room.
- **Bot notes:** Farigiraf's Armor Tail blocks priority moves, including Fake Out, so an opposing Rillaboom cannot reset terrain and Fake Out Indeedee in the same turn. Charizard-Y is for water and weather matchups (rain, sand); against some teams, bring straight Trick Room and no Mega.
